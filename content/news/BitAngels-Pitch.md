We're proud to share that Sundial Protocol was selected this week as one of the final two projects by BitAngels in London, giving us the opportunity to present to the Global BitAngels network.

Sundial Protocol contributor Robert Gaskell pitched Sundial at the BitAngels event hosted by Brown Rudnick LLP., where there was an enthusiastic response to our value proposition as the world's first institutional-grade programmable layer built on Bitcoin, enabling BTC yield generation at scale. 

With a lead investor secured and our Seed round nearing completion, along with significant BTC commitments already pledged, we’re excited about the momentum building around Sundial as we continue to grow at pace.
