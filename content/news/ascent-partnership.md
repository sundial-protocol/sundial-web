Sundial Protocol is proud to announce its partnership with Ascent Rivals, the Cardano-based, adrenaline-fuelled racing game pushing the boundaries of blockchain-powered esports.

This collaboration highlights the versatility of Sundial’s UTXO Layer-2 infrastructure beyond traditional finance, demonstrating how secure and scalable DeFi technology can fuel innovation in entertainment and gaming. By integrating with Ascent Rivals, Sundial showcases a new dimension of UTXO chain utility — where digital assets, in-game economies, and immersive experiences converge on a trustless foundation.

For Sundial, this partnership is connecting institutional-grade infrastructure with mainstream use cases. Ascent Rivals offers an ideal arena to prove how Layer-2 scalability can support high-throughput applications like esports.

Together, Sundial and Ascent Rivals are bringing DeFi technology into the hands of players and communities worldwide.
