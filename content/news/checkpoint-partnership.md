Trust is the foundation on which strong communities and partnerships are built. Check Point’s proven verification tools enhance confidence among both institutional players and retail users entering Bitcoin DeFi. By adopting these standards, Sundial continues to raise the bar for transparency and reliability, paving the way for deeper collaborations across the industry.

## Foundation for Scalable Growth

By integrating Check Point’s technology, we are laying the groundwork for rapid expansion into new markets and products. With a stronger infrastructure in place, Sundial is poised to scale while maintaining the safeguards that protect our users and our mission.

At Sundial, our mission has always been to build an and innovative ecosystem for Bitcoin-powered decentralised finance.
