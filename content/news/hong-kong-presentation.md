Sheldon, CEO of Sundial, presented the protocol's vision at Waffle's flagship event in Hong Kong, highlighting how Sundial is building trustless infrastructure to bring Bitcoin into Cardano's DeFi ecosystem. On a panel alongside leading voices in cross-chain finance, Sheldon spoke on the importance of ZK-powered bridges and institutional-ready design. The session sparked thoughtful discussion around Bitcoin's next chapter - moving from passive store of value to programmable, yield-bearing asset.
