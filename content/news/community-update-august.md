## Generate Sustainable Returns on Bitcoin Holdings

Sundial enables Bitcoin holders, including corporates and institutions, to generate yield without giving up custody or security. Our non-custodial environment ensures that assets remain fully in user control, with sustainable returns driven by protocol activity rather than synthetic yield schemes.


#### Specifications:

100% Non-Custodial – Assets remain in users’ control


Targetting up to 2–5% rewards for staking – Yield from protocol activity


0% Bridge Risk – Trustless rollup architecture with no wrapped assets or custodial intermediaries
