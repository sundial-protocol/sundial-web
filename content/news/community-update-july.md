## SUNDIAL COMPLETES THE SILICON VALLEY DRAPER UNIVERSITY PROGRAM

We are proud to announce that the Sundial Team officially completed the Draper University Founder Residency Program this summer, marking a significant milestone in our mission to seamlessly bring DeFi to Bitcoin . Over five intensive weeks in Silicon Valley, the Sundial team built powerful strategic partnerships with key players across the Cardano and Web3 ecosystems and accelerated the development of our innovative UTxO Layer 2 solution. We express our gratitude to Catalyst, Draper University, and Yaad Labs for their support.

## BTC L2S TAKE THE CENTRE STAGE AT BTC PRAGUE

BTC Prague 2025, the most notable event of its kind in Europe, gathered over 10,000 Bitcoin enthusiasts – and Layer 2s were at the forefront. Micheal Saylor highlighted the transformative potential of Layer 2s during his keynote, stating that “it will be moving at the speed of light on layer 2s, on layer 3s... trading between billions of nodes.” Sundial’s layer 2 design, secured by zero-knowledge proofs, full UTxO security, and institutional readiness, is undeniably a part of this new emerging era of Bitcoin.
