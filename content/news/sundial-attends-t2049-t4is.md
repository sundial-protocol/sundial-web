Sundial recently participated in two major global events, TOKEN2049 Singapore and the Tech for Impact Summit in Tokyo — each showcasing the future of blockchain, finance, and technology’s role in building a more inclusive digital economy.

At TOKEN2049 Singapore 2025, Sundial joined leading voices from across the digital asset ecosystem including institutional investors and DeFi pioneers to infrastructure builders and policymakers. This year’s conference focused on the evolving landscape of Bitcoin-based innovation, institutional adoption, and the integration of real-world assets (RWA) into blockchain systems.

The event also highlighted how interoperability frameworks and zero-knowledge technologies are shaping a more scalable, secure and regulated crypto environment. For Sundial, it was an opportunity to connect directly with partners, investors, and developers advancing this mission and demonstrating how its Bitcoin Layer-2 architecture is unlocking programmable yield and cross-chain liquidity built on verifiable security and transparency.

Across discussions and side events, the message was clear: Bitcoin DeFi is entering a new phase which is one driven by credible infrastructure and institutional engagement. Sundial’s work sits at the centre of this transformation, building infrastructure that extends Bitcoin’s utility beyond a store of value into a foundation for a programmable financial system.

Following Singapore, the team attended the Tech for Impact Summit in Tokyo, an event uniting innovators, policymakers, and technologists who are using blockchain, AI, and advanced data systems to drive social and environmental impact.

The Summit explored how these technologies can create transparency in global supply chains, enhance sustainability tracking, and expand access to fair financial systems.

For Sundial, the Tech for Impact Summit reinforced the broader relevance of blockchain beyond finance: as a tool for inclusion and accountability. The company’s presence underscored its commitment to advancing the responsible use of decentralised technology in service of a more resilient and equitable world.

As both events made clear, the convergence of innovation, interoperability, and impact will define the next era of digital transformation. Sundial remains at the forefront of this movement by building the bridges between Bitcoin and institutions.
